Privacy Policy

This is a template policy for the first release. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional for your jurisdiction before launch.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and a securely hashed password.
  • Delivery addresses you save, and the address used for each order.
  • Group memberships, quantities ordered, payments, refunds, complaints, photos you upload and reviews.
  • For vendors: business details and verification documents.
  • Technical logs such as IP addresses for security and audit purposes.

How we use it

To run buying groups, fulfil and deliver orders, verify payments, handle complaints and refunds, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Who can see it

  • Other group members see only your name — never your address, phone number or individual order.
  • The vendor awarded an order sees what it needs to fulfil it: quantities and, for home delivery, your delivery address and phone number.
  • Vendor documents, private bids, photos and addresses are never available through public links.
  • Administrators access data only to operate the service; sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log.

Your choices

You can update your profile and addresses at any time. To request a copy or deletion of your data, contact us. Some records (orders, payments) must be kept for accounting purposes.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies for sign-in sessions and form security.
