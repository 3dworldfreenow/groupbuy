About {{ app_name }}

We help neighbours buy fresh vegetables and fruit together, so households get bulk quality and suppliers get reliable, consolidated demand.

Our approach

  • Transparent pricing. Every order shows produce, delivery and fee components separately.
  • Fair competition. Offers are sealed until the deadline and scored with a published policy where price is only one factor.
  • Quality as a process. Grades, batch records, inspections, delivery condition and complaints are all recorded.
  • Honest labelling. We distinguish vendor-declared information from platform-verified information and never make organic or pesticide-free claims without evidence.

Two stages

We start by operating the first vendor ourselves — sourcing, checking, packing and coordinating delivery. As the community grows, verified external vendors compete for group orders under exactly the same rules. Our own vendor account is always disclosed as platform-owned.
